## Top Disease Registry Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Clinical Registries, Condition Tracking, Population Health Surveillance, Outcomes Measurement & Specialty Data Collection*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Disease Registry** systems. These platforms collect, manage, and analyze structured clinical data for specific conditions or populations—supporting quality improvement, research, public health surveillance, and value-based care reporting.



**Examples** include FIGmd, MedeAnalytics, Health Catalyst, Premier PINC AI, Arcadia, Azara DRVS, Epic Healthy Planet, Oracle Population Health, Wellcentive, and MDLand (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Dedicated commercial disease-registry products dominate in the US. Open options are strongest in global health and standards-based stacks—**OpenMRS**, **OpenSRP**, **DHIS2**, **FHIR**-native tools, and facility registries. This section is expanded with every major relevant project found.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[FIGmd](https://www.figmd.com/)**  

  Clinical data registry platform supporting specialty societies and quality reporting with abstraction and analytics.



- **[MedeAnalytics, Health Catalyst, Premier PINC AI, Arcadia](https://medeanalytics.com/)**  

  Population health and analytics platforms that power condition-specific registries, quality measures, and outcomes tracking.



- **[Azara DRVS, Wellcentive, MDLand](https://www.azarahealthcare.com/)**  

  Registry and quality-reporting tools oriented toward ambulatory, FQHC, and value-based care organizations.



- **[Epic Healthy Planet, Oracle Population Health](https://www.epic.com/)**  

  EHR-embedded population health modules used to maintain disease registries and care-management cohorts.



- **[Other commercial disease registry platforms](https://www.figmd.com/)**  

  Additional specialty-society and research-registry solutions for clinical data collection and reporting.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenMRS](https://openmrs.org/)**  

  Leading open-source electronic medical records platform—highly configurable for condition programs (HIV, TB, NCD, etc.) and used as a foundation for disease-specific data collection worldwide.



- **[OpenSRP](https://opensrp.io/)**  

  Open-source smart register platform (FHIR-native)—supports frontline registers and electronic immunization/disease registries with offline capability.



- **[DHIS2](https://github.com/dhis2)**  

  Widely deployed open-source health information system—aggregate and individual data for surveillance, program monitoring, and national disease registries.



- **[Global Open Facility Registry (GOFR)](https://github.com/intrahealth/gofr)**  

  Open facility registry tool on FHIR/mCSD—supports hierarchical facility data that underpins registry and surveillance systems.



- **[FHIR server & registry patterns (HAPI FHIR)](https://github.com/hapifhir/hapi-fhir)**  

  Open FHIR server commonly used as the backend for condition-specific registries and cohort management.



- **[OpenHIE / OpenMRS disease-program implementations](https://github.com/search?q=OpenMRS+HIV+OR+OpenMRS+registry)**  

  Community distributions and modules tailored to specific disease programs and longitudinal registers.



- **[Research cohort & registry tooling](https://github.com/search?q=disease+registry+OR+clinical+registry+FHIR+open+source)**  

  FHIR-based and custom open projects for research registries, outcomes tracking, and cohort definition.



- **[Bahmni & OpenMRS-based hospital systems](https://www.bahmni.org/)**  

  Open distributions that include configurable clinical programs usable as specialty or disease registers.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **EMR-as-registry**: OpenMRS and Bahmni configured for longitudinal disease programs.

- **Frontline registers**: OpenSRP for community and facility smart registers.

- **Aggregate surveillance**: DHIS2 for national and program-level disease monitoring.

- **Facility master data**: GOFR for registry infrastructure.

- **Composable stacks**: HAPI FHIR + OpenMRS/OpenSRP + DHIS2 reporting + open analytics (Superset/Metabase).

- Commercial US disease-registry products still lead in specialty-society abstraction, quality-measure engines, and payer reporting.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Combine **OpenMRS** or **OpenSRP** (data capture), **HAPI FHIR** (interoperability), **DHIS2** (aggregate reporting), and **Superset/Metabase** (dashboards).  

Commercial platforms (FIGmd, Health Catalyst, Arcadia, Epic Healthy Planet, etc.) provide abstraction services and US quality-program depth.  

Global health programs often run fully open stacks; US specialty registries typically rely on commercial platforms. Open components are production-proven in many countries when properly implemented.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Disease registries handle sensitive clinical data and may support research, quality reporting, or public health. Systems must comply with HIPAA, GDPR, and applicable research or reporting regulations. Data quality, consent, and governance are critical.

- Open-source tools offer control and adaptability but require clinical, technical, and compliance ownership. Commercial platforms shift abstraction, measure logic, and support to the vendor. Neither replaces proper registry governance, data stewardship, or institutional review where required.
